workshop reflection

Through weekly workshops, I experiment with different digital methods, including building this portfolio website in two ways: with a content management system (CampusPress) and with code (HTML, CSS and GitHub Pages). Alongside the practical work, I engage with academic readings to question what each method reveals about digital media, and what it leaves out. This site documents that process, with weekly reflections on what I did, what I learned and how it connects to the module’s key ideas.
